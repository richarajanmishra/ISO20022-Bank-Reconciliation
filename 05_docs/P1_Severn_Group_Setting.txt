1. About Severn Group
Suggested: Severn Group is a fictional UK-headquartered insurance services group with three entities. It reports in GBP. All data in this project is dummy data created for a portfolio simulation.

2. Entities

Severn UK (GBP): insurance services, policyholder and corporate customers
Severn US (USD): sales of services to US customers
Severn India (INR): shared services centre (not part of Project 1)

3. Bank accounts in scope

Account	Entity	Currency	Used for
GB29SEVN60161331926819	Severn UK	GBP	Customer receipts, supplier payments, claims
GB54SEVN60161331926820	Severn UK	EUR	Receipts from European customers
US11SEVN00012345678901	Severn US	USD	Customer receipts and supplier payments

4. Cash flows

Money in: customer receipts against invoices (INV- references)
Money out: supplier payments (PAY- references) and policyholder claim payments

5. Scope of this project
Daily reconciliation of the three bank accounts for 5–9 October 2026 using ISO 20022 camt.053 statements, matched to the cash ledger. Each account is reconciled in its own currency; balances are converted to GBP only for the group cash position.
